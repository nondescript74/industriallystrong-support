Reczipes2

Recipes from photos and links, with food-sensitivity flags.

Give Reczipes2 a photo or a link to a recipe and it extracts the ingredients, instructions and images, answers questions about it, and saves it for later. Ingredients can go to your Reminders shopping list, and a profile of your food sensitivities, allergens and FODMAP categories flags recipes that may be a risk.

Questions

Why does the app ask for a Claude API key?

Recipe extraction uses Anthropic's Claude. You get your own API key from Anthropic and enter it in the app's settings, where it is stored in the device's Keychain.

How do I share recipes?

Share a recipe or a whole book from its menu; the other person opens it in their own copy of the app.

Are the allergy and FODMAP flags medical advice?

No. They are a guide based on the ingredient list; always check labels and follow your own care advice.

Requirements

  • iPhone, iPad or Mac with a current OS
  • Your own Claude API key for extraction
  • iCloud account to sync and share
